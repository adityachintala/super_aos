- Computing has moved from centralized machines to **parallel and distributed systems** that are data-intensive and network-centric. **HPC** pursues raw speed; **HTC** pursues tasks completed per unit time.
- **Distributed** means autonomous computers with private memory exchanging messages. A **cloud** is a pool of virtualized resources that may be centralized or distributed.
- Four system classes: **clusters** (homogeneous, local, usually with multiple system images unless SSI software is added), **P2P networks**, **grids** and **clouds**.
- **IaaS** rents infrastructure, **PaaS** rents a platform for your own applications, **SaaS** rents finished software.
- **Amdahl**: S = 1 / (α + (1 − α)/n), bounded by 1/α. Efficiency E = S/n collapses on large clusters for a fixed workload. **Gustafson**: S′ = α + (1 − α)n when the workload is scaled.
- **Availability** = MTTF / (MTTF + MTTR). Avoid single points of failure.
- **Energy** is saved by switching off idle servers, by energy-aware design at each layer, and by **DVFS**.
